<section class="section" id="research">
  <h2 class="section-title"><span class="num">02 /</span> My Research</h2>
  <p class="kicker">How does the gut grow up?</p>
  <p>The gastrointestinal tract is far from finishing development at birth. During the first years of life it keeps
  developing and adapting as the body meets new foods and a changing environment.</p>
  <p>Under the supervision of <strong>Prof. Shalev Itzkovitz</strong>, I study the
  <strong>development of the gastrointestinal tract during the first years of life</strong>.</p>

  <h3>From lactose to the developing gut</h3>
  <p>My path has always led through the digestive system: from how we digest food, to how the organ
  that digests it is built in the first place.</p>
  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card rose"><span class="card-icon">🥛</span> <span class="card-title">M.Sc. · Lactose intolerance:</span> <span class="card-text">Why some of us can't digest milk sugar, and what happens in the gut when we can't.</span></div>
    <div class="card teal"><span class="card-icon">🌱</span> <span class="card-title">PhD · Gut development:</span> <span class="card-text">How the gastrointestinal tract develops and matures during the first years of life.</span></div>
  </div>

  <h3>Research interests</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Developmental biology:</strong> how organs form and mature after birth</li>
    <li><strong>The digestive system:</strong> the link between gut structure and how we digest food</li>
